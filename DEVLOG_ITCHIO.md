# Jett & Sage Join the Fight: Skirmish Update

The action in **Valorant 2D** is growing with two playable agents and a new team-based mode. Choose how you want to enter the fight: master movement and timing with Jett, protect and support your team with Sage, or test your decisions in Skirmish.

## Jett: speed and control

Jett arrives with her full set of playable abilities:

- **Cloudburst (C):** throw a smoke cloud that blocks vision and can be curved in flight.
- **Updraft (Q):** launch yourself upward to gain height and create new movement options.
- **Tailwind (E):** prepare a gust and quickly dash in the chosen direction.
- **Blade Storm (X):** equip throwing knives for precise attacks; alternate fire launches a burst.

Combine her abilities to move across the map, break lines of sight, and reposition before your opponent can react.

## Sage: support and defense

Sage brings tools to support her team and control routes:

- **Barrier Orb (C):** create a wall that blocks passage and helps secure positions.
- **Slow Orb (Q):** deploy an area that slows anyone who crosses it.
- **Healing Orb (E):** restore health to Sage or an ally.
- **Resurrection (X):** bring a fallen teammate back into the fight.

The barrier can change how an area is contested, while healing and resurrection help your team keep up the pressure.

## Skirmish: rounds between two teams

The new Skirmish mode pits two teams against each other in configurable **1v1 or 2v2** matches. Each round begins with a preparation phase, continues with the fight, and ends with a break before the next round. The first team to reach **10 round wins** takes the match.

Play locally against bots or create and join **LAN** rooms. In LAN matches, the host configures the room. The dedicated map includes spawn points and barriers that come down when preparation ends.

## Also available

- Deathmatch for practice and combat.
- Jett, Phoenix, and Sage as playable agents.
- An arsenal featuring the Vandal, Classic, knife, Spectre, Operator, Judge, and Odin.
- Movement, shooting, reloading, health, shields, and weapon selection.
- Main menu, network lobby, and in-game HUD.

## Additional improvements

- Refined Skirmish rounds with configurable bot difficulty, team selection, round results, and a scoreboard for both teams.
- Improved LAN room setup so the host can configure the match and share the room address with other players.
- Added round preparation barriers that open when the fight begins, plus ability resets between rounds.
- Added spectator controls for following teammates after being eliminated.
- Polished combat feedback with kill streaks, damage indicators, ability timers, and healing and resurrection effects.
